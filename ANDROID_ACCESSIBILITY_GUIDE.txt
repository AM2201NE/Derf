DERF PQ MESSENGER — ANDROID ACCESSIBILITY & AUTOMATIC ENCRYPTION GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how to enable Android Accessibility Services and automatic clipboard
decryption monitoring for seamless in-place text selection encryption on Android.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ENABLING ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE ON ANDROID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
2. Go to "Accessibility" -> "Downloaded Services" or "Installed Services".
3. Tap "Derf Accessibility Service" and toggle it ON.
4. Allow full permissions when prompted by Android.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AUTOMATIC CLIPBOARD & TEXT SELECTION MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When running Derf on Android:
• The app includes an active background clipboard thread (`_clipboard_monitor_loop`)
  that automatically scans for incoming `DERF:V1:` ciphertext packets copied from any chat app.
• When a ciphertext packet is copied, the app automatically decrypts the message and alerts you.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. IN-PLACE SELECTION & TEXT REPLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• When typing or selecting text in any Android application (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, SMS):
  1. Select the confidential text you want to encrypt.
  2. Tap "Copy".
  3. Switch to Derf or tap "ENCRYPT" in the notification/clipboard overlay.
  4. The encrypted `DERF:V1:` packet is placed back on your clipboard ready to paste!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
