DERF PQ MESSENGER — COMPLETE ANDROID USER & DEPLOYMENT GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how to build, launch, and use Derf PQ Messenger on your Android phone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: DEPLOYING & LAUNCHING ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE (POWERSHELL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Run these 3 commands in PowerShell in your `C:\Users\GG\Desktop\Derf` folder:

1. Clean the old build cache:
    Remove-Item -Recurse -Force build\derf\android

2. Re-create the Android build package:
    briefcase create android

3. Build, install, and run directly on your connected phone:
    briefcase run android -d f6951a59

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: HOW TO USE DERF ON ANDROID (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. UNLOCKING / CREATING YOUR VAULT:
   • On app startup, enter a Master Vault Password and tap "UNLOCK VAULT" or "CREATE VAULT".
   • Vault storage is encrypted using ChaCha20Poly1305 + Argon2/PBKDF2 key derivation.

2. ADDING A CONTACT:
   • Tap the "➕ Contact" button in the upper right.
   • Enter a Handle (e.g., "Alice") and paste her ML-KEM-768 public key or identity bundle.
   • Tap "SAVE CONTACT".

3. PAIRING & KEY EXCHANGE (HANDSHAKE):
   • Option A (Generate Invite): Tap "➕ Gen Invite". The handshake invite code is automatically
     copied to your clipboard. Send this code to your contact via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.
   • Option B (Accept Invite): When your contact sends you an invite, copy it to your clipboard
     and tap "📥 Accept Invite". The handshake reply code is copied to your clipboard. Send it back.
   • Option C (Complete Pairing): When you receive the handshake reply back, copy it to your
     clipboard and tap "✅ Complete Pair". Your Double Ratchet session is now active!

4. ENCRYPTING CONFIDENTIAL MESSAGES:
   • Select your contact as the Active Peer.
   • Type your message in the text box at the bottom.
   • Tap "ENCRYPT".
   • The message is compressed with Alien Stack (Zstd/Zlib) and encrypted into a uniform-sized
     DERF:V1: packet.
   • The ciphertext packet is automatically copied to your phone's clipboard!
   • Paste and send this packet through any messaging app or social network (Instagram, Discord, etc.).

5. DECRYPTING RECEIVED MESSAGES:
   • When you receive an encrypted packet (`DERF:V1:...`), copy it to your clipboard or paste it
     into the "Paste DERF:V1: ciphertext packet here..." box.
   • Tap "DECRYPT".
   • The decrypted plaintext appears in your chat window and in an alert popup!
