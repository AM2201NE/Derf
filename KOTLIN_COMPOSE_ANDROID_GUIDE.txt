--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO BUILD & RUN IN ANDROID STUDIO:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch Android Studio on Windows.
2. Click `File` -> `Open` and select the folder:
   `C:\Users\GG\Desktop\Derf\android`
3. Wait for Gradle sync to complete. Chaquopy will automatically download pre-built
   Python 3.12 wheel dependencies (`cryptography`, `kyber-py`, `zstandard`).
4. Connect your Android phone via USB (or start an Android Emulator).
5. Click the green `Run 'app'` button (Shift + F10) in Android Studio.
6. The native Jetpack Compose app will build, deploy, and launch on your phone!
